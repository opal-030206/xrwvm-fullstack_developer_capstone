About Cars Dealership

A national car retailer built on straight answers, fair prices and people who know cars. Eighteen branches, one standard of service.

Who we are

Cars Dealership started in Kansas City in 1998 with a single lot and a simple idea: publish the real price, show the real history report, and let customers decide without pressure. Today we operate dealerships in fifteen states and sell new and pre-owned vehicles from eight manufacturers. Every branch runs the same systems, the same inspection checklist and the same promise — if something is not right, we fix it.

How we work

Three rules that have not changed since 1998. Published prices, a free vehicle history report on every used car, and a seven-day exchange window. Our reviews are written by verified account holders and scored automatically, so nothing is edited before you read it.
